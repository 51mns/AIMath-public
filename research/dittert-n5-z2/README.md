# Dittert n=5 — two-zero matching exclusion

**Claim ID:** `C-DITTERT-N5-Z2-MATCHING-EXCLUSION`  
**Canonical level:** `INDEPENDENTLY_REPRODUCED`

No global Dittert maximizer for `n=5` has, up to independent row and column permutations, exactly two zero entries lying in distinct rows and distinct columns.

Equivalently, the full `N5-Z2-MATCHING` support orbit is excluded.

The proof uses fixed-zero-pattern symmetrisation, the zero-entry KKT chamber inequality, and only two positive-entry stationarity equalities. Those two equalities force the same symmetry coordinate `x` to satisfy both `x<0` and `x>0`.

## Boundary

This is a support-class exclusion, not a proof of the full Dittert `n=5` conjecture and not a publication-novelty claim.
