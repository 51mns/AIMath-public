## What the campaign achieved

The programme progressed from exact small-rank structure to general mathematics:

1. exactly three independent ternary moves were proved to have only induced 4- and 6-cycles;
2. an explicit all-rank carry family attained the sharp component-diameter bound;
3. rank 3 was reduced to a finite masked core and completely classified into 24 connected unlabeled types, all partial cubes;
4. rank 4 supplied a sharp counterexample boundary rather than a forced positive generalisation;
5. arbitrary fixed move rank was reduced losslessly to a finite `Q_r` core with legality masks;
6. the rank-3 class received graph-intrinsic and COM/OM classifications;
7. rank-saturation rigidity linked B3RCC move count to intrinsic partial-cube rank;
8. an external APC target was isolated;
9. the remaining five-move B3RCC APC sector was closed by a theorem for arbitrary antipodal partial cubes;
10. the rank-4 APC order bound was generalised to the all-rank theorem in `APC_ALL_RANK_THEOREM.md`.

## Why the theme is on HOLD

The closest audited external target was the generalized antipodal-partial-cube minimum-degree question `delta(G)<=rho(G)`. AIMath's all-rank theorem is a global order lower bound in terms of rank and isometric dimension. The audit did not find a non-tautological source-backed bridge from a hypothetical high minimum degree to an incompatible order/isometric-dimension upper restriction.

Therefore the accepted theorem is reusable mathematics, but it is not currently load-bearing for that closest external target.
