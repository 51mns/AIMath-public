# Primary-source audit

## Springborn — decisive source

Boris Springborn, *The worst approximable rational numbers*, Journal of Number Theory 263 (2024), 153–205, DOI `10.1016/j.jnt.2024.04.013`. Final arXiv version inspected in the private audit: arXiv:2209.15542v3 (27 May 2024).

Locations used by the accepted proof:

- p. 1, equation (1): rational approximation constant `C(x)`;
- pp. 5–6, Definition 2.1, equations (6)–(7): left/right companion formula and recurrence;
- p. 6, Theorem 2.2: `C(x) >= 1/3` iff `x` is a Markov fraction or a companion;
- p. 2, equation (4): invariance under `x -> +/-x+n`, distinguished from general `GL_2(Z)` invariance.

These statements are sufficient for `PROOF.md`.

## Bombieri — continued-fraction background

Enrico Bombieri, *Continued fractions and the Markoff tree*, Expositiones Mathematicae 25 (2007), 187–213, DOI `10.1016/j.exmath.2006.10.002`.

The audited publisher text, Appendix A, equations (A.1)–(A.11), contains the matrix/convergent formalism, determinant identity and approximation identity used as background. The public proof itself needs only the elementary convergent identity.
