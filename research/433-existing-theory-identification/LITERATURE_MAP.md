This public export records only formulas supported by the frozen private source audit.

## Enrico Bombieri

Enrico Bombieri, *Continued fractions and the Markoff tree*, Expositiones Mathematicae 25 (2007), 187–213, DOI `10.1016/j.exmath.2006.10.002`.

Used as classical background for Cohn words/matrices, continued-fraction matrix actions and determinant identities. The main identification proof does not depend on an inferred formula absent from the source.

## J. O. Button

J. O. Button, *Markoff Numbers, Principal Ideals and Continued Fraction Expansions*, Journal of Number Theory 87 (2001), 77–95, DOI `10.1006/jnth.2000.2578`.

The frozen audit confirmed bibliographic metadata but did not retrieve the article body. Therefore no root formula, representative convention or theorem number is attributed to that inaccessible body. Whether the factor-5 CRT representative appears there under another name remains unresolved.

## Boris Springborn

Boris Springborn, *The worst approximable rational numbers*, Journal of Number Theory 263 (2024), 153–205, DOI `10.1016/j.jnt.2024.04.013`; final arXiv version `arXiv:2209.15542v3`.

The accepted audit used the Markov-fraction tree, companion classification and integer-affine symmetry framework. The source also provides first-pair fingerprints compatible with the fixed-433 ray.

## A. P. Veselov

A. P. Veselov, *Markov fractions and Cohn matrices*, `arXiv:2604.17401v1` (19 Apr 2026).

Proof-relevant locations in the frozen audit:

- Proposition 2.1, equations (6)–(9): oriented cross-determinant identities for local Markov-fraction triples;
- equations (12)–(15), Theorem 3.1: `C_t(a)`, `I_t(a)` and `mu(t)=I_t(0)`;
- the ordered Cohn multiplication recursion used on the fixed Farey ray.

These formulas supply the exact `mu <-> Cohn-index` bridge needed in `PROOF.md`.
