## Result

A concrete three-way correspondence was fixed between finite simple
continued fractions, Euler continuants, and matching partition functions on
caterpillar/backbone paths.

The graph cut at one changed vertex yields a reusable exact finite
sensitivity formula for changing one partial quotient. The formula and the
full state/operation/invariant mapping passed exact held-out verification.

The underlying continuant↔caterpillar matching correspondence is known in
Hosoya (2007), and a coordinate derivative equivalent to the infinitesimal
version of the sensitivity law appears in Puri et al. (NeurIPS 2021,
Supplement Lemma 2). Therefore this worker does not claim novelty.
